## Why Care?

No one uses the chroma decks any more, so keeping them as a live client site
only added upkeep: lockfile refreshes, security alerts, a Vercel project to
keep green. It's now listed as a former client site.

***

## What's New?

- **The submodule is removed** from `.gitmodules` and `pnpm-workspace.yaml`.
- **`AGENTS.md` lists it under "Former client sites"**, and FILEMAP's
  client-site rows and tree drop it.
- **Nothing is deleted.** `lossless-group/chroma-decks` stays on GitHub at
  `a2d5323` (all three branches), already on Astro 7.4.13 and shell 0.4.1.
  Its last Vercel deployments succeeded, and the project is untouched. A full
  local checkout, including the ignored `.env`, `auth.db` and the 31 MB
  `corpus/` of management-supplied material, which was never on GitHub, is
  archived outside the tree.

To bring it back, re-add it with `git submodule add
git@github.com:lossless-group/chroma-decks.git client-sites/chroma-decks`
and restore the ignored files from the archive.
