# DidiDecks AI

> {{SITE_NAME}}

A code-first slide-deck system for materials that have to survive due diligence: the fund decks, board updates and data rooms an LP, co-investor or acquirer will check line by line. Decks are repositories, slides are components, variants are first-class, and AI does most of the authoring. Built by The Lossless Group as part of its `ai-labs` workspace.

This site holds {{CHANGELOG_COUNT}} changelog entries (what shipped) and {{CONTEXTV_COUNT}} context-v docs (specs, plans, explorations and blueprints) from {{REPO_COUNT}} public repos: `dididecks-ai` itself and its public packages. Client deck repos are private and never appear here.
